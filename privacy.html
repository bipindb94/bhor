Privacy Policy: BHOR Uploader

Last updated: 23 October 2026

BHOR Uploader is a private tool used only by the Bhojpuriya Rang (BHOR) team to upload and manage videos on the team's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the tool uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, posts comments, and reads the channel's own video statistics and analytics.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data from viewers or any other Google user.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use the data for advertising.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the team's own computer and can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Through the BHOR YouTube channel.
